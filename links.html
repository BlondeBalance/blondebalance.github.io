How to use this page

Put it in your bio — one link, everything inside

  1. Copy this page's link.
  2. Open TikTok and go to your Profile.
  3. Tap Edit profile, then Website.
  4. Paste the link and tap Save.
  5. Done — this page is now your link tree. Every button above is one tap away for your followers.

Opening the links

  1. Tap any button and it opens right away in a new tab.
  2. If a tap doesn't open it, press & hold the button — you'll get the option to open the direct link.
  3. Still stuck? Press & hold, copy the link, and paste it into your browser.
